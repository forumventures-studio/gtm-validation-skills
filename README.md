# GTM Validation Skills for Claude

Four Claude skills for validating a startup idea through real buyer conversations. Built by Draper Sturdivant, GTM Lead at Forum Ventures Studio, and used across 20 studio companies.

## The skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [ICP Scoring](icp-scoring/SKILL.md) | Scores a list of contacts 1 to 10 against your ICP, so you know who to reach first. |
| [Messaging](messaging/SKILL.md) | Writes a connection note and two follow-ups in your voice: a signal you noticed, why it matters, then a soft ask. |
| [Prospect List Builder](prospect-list-builder/SKILL.md) | Turns your ICP into job titles, company filters, where to find people, and a ready-to-fill CSV. |
| [Signal-to-Angle](signal-to-angle/SKILL.md) | Turns a prospect's recent post, news, or job change into a reason to reach out and an opening line. |

## How to use them

1. Download this repo, or just the skill folders you want.
2. Add them to Claude as skills, or keep them in a Claude project.
3. Ask Claude to use one, like "Use my ICP Scoring skill," and paste in your contacts.
