# Signal-to-Angle

Find the reason to reach out right now, and write the first line.

## Before you start

Read these files from the project:
- **Buying Signals.md**: the top signals and their angles, weak signals, and fake signals
- **Voice.md**: how the user writes
- **ICP.md**: the angle that matters to each role

## Input

Something recent about a prospect: a LinkedIn post, a news article, a job change, a funding announcement, or a job listing.

## Output

1. **The signal:** what changed, in one line. Name which signal from Buying Signals.md it matches, or say it's a new one worth adding.
2. **Signal strength:** strong, weak, or fake, using the rankings in Buying Signals.md, with a one-line reason.
3. **The angle:** why this is a good moment to reach out, in one or two lines. Connect the change to the problem it usually creates.
4. **The opening line:** one sentence in the user's voice, using Voice.md, that references the signal specifically.

## Weak or fake signals

If the signal is weak or fake per Buying Signals.md, say so, skip the angle and the opening line, and suggest waiting for a stronger signal. A forced opener does more harm than no message.

## Rules

- Reference something real and specific. Never generic flattery, like "Loved your post."
- No hype, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
- Draft only. The user approves before anything is sent.
